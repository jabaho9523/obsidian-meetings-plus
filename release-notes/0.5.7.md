Two bug fixes, both reported by users. Thank you for the clear reports.

**Your daily-note meeting notes are no longer overwritten**

If a calendar writes meeting notes into the daily note, clicking the same meeting a second time used to replace its section with a fresh template, erasing anything you'd written. Now a second click just opens the daily note at that meeting's section and leaves your text alone. Thanks @mxmehl ([#24](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/issues/24)).

**"Run Templater on new notes" works**

The Templater integration called Templater incorrectly and failed with `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'start_templater_task')`, so the option never actually worked. It does now, and the README has a new **Templater** section with a working example. Thanks @Segdon ([#41](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/issues/41)) and @t3mujin ([#25](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/issues/25)), who both included the full error. That made this a quick fix.

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. Settings, calendars and templates carry over untouched.
