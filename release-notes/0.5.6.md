Two fixes contributed by [@mrueg](https://github.com/mrueg), plus internal hardening.

**Daylight saving time**

Day arithmetic throughout the plugin stepped by a fixed 24 hours, which breaks on the days the clocks change. On a 25-hour day the date-picker grid could duplicate or skip a day, the previous/next day arrows could appear to do nothing, and meetings starting between 23:00 and midnight could drop out of the daily-note list. All day math is now calendar-based. Thanks @mrueg ([#17](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/17)).

**Faster meeting lookups**

Checking whether a meeting already had a note meant scanning every markdown file in the vault — on every render of the sidebar. That is now an incrementally maintained index, which is noticeably faster on large vaults. Thanks @mrueg ([#18](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/18)).

Creating a meeting note still performs an authoritative check before writing a file, so even a stale index can never produce a duplicate note.

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. Settings, calendars, and templates carry over untouched.
