**Look back up to a year**

You can now browse past meetings up to 365 days back (previously 30). Set **Settings → Meetings Plus → Look-back window (days)**. Older days load a few seconds after you change it, with no restart needed.

Some calendars only include recent history in their feed — Outlook's published calendar links, for example — so how far back you can actually see depends on your calendar.

Recurring meetings are expanded across the whole window, so even with a full year of look-back every upcoming occurrence still shows.

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. The default look-back stays at 7 days.
