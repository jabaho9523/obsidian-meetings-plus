**Tasks and questions straight from your meeting notes**

Type `T:` for a task or `Q:` for a question while the meeting runs. Meetings Plus turns tasks into checkboxes and keeps track of the questions nobody answered yet. The sidebar shows everything still open from the last two weeks, and you can tick tasks off right there.

Turn it on in **Settings → Meetings Plus → Harvest**.

**Plays well with others**

Your meeting tasks also show up in [Tasks Plus](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/tasks-plus). If you use HyperCap on macOS, you get one shared note per meeting instead of two.

**Also:** monthly meetings like "the 2nd Tuesday" are now described correctly. Thanks @OpenDeernet ([#54](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/54)).
