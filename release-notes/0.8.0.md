**Harvest: tasks and questions from your notes**

Write `T: Send the deck to Bo` or `Q: Who owns the budget?` in a meeting note. Meetings Plus turns each `T:` line into a checkbox and keeps a list of the open questions in the note's properties. Answer a question with an indented `A:` line under it, and it drops off the list.

Harvest is off until you turn it on in **Settings → Meetings Plus → Harvest**.

- It runs when you leave a note, or when you press **Harvest this note** in the sidebar. The button works on any note.
- Choose where it runs by itself: only notes Meetings Plus made, or any note in folders you pick.
- **Create meetings overview** (command) makes a Base that lists every meeting with open tasks or questions.
- The sidebar shows how many tasks are open under each meeting, and an **Open tasks** list you can tick off.
- The short ids Harvest adds to task lines stay hidden while you write.

**Works with Tasks Plus**

Harvested tasks are ordinary checkboxes, so they also show up in [Tasks Plus](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/tasks-plus), our free plugin for Today, Upcoming and Anytime lists. Use Tasks Plus 0.1.4 or later.

**Works with HyperCap**

If you also use HyperCap on macOS, both apps open the same note for a meeting instead of making two.

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. Your notes stay as they are until you turn Harvest on.
