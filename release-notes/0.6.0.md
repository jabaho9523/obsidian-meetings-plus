A big release, almost entirely contributed by [@mrueg](https://github.com/mrueg), with a first contribution from [@jarno-lernou-skyline](https://github.com/jarno-lernou-skyline). Thank you both.

**Recurring meetings show the right details**

If you moved or renamed a single occurrence of a recurring meeting in your calendar, Meetings Plus still showed the series' original title, location and attendees. It now reads that occurrence's own details. This also fixes a case where an edited occurrence could be counted twice, or could rename an unrelated note. Thanks @mrueg ([#36](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/36)).

**Correct times across daylight-saving changes**

A recurring meeting created in a different time zone could show up an hour early, and on the wrong day, after a daylight-saving switch. Thanks @mrueg ([#37](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/37)).

**All-day and early-morning meetings filed under the right date**

East of UTC — all of Europe, for example — every all-day event and any meeting early in the morning was internally keyed to the previous day. Notes are now keyed by your local date. Existing notes and daily-note sections are recognised automatically and updated the first time you open them, so nothing is duplicated. Thanks @mrueg ([#38](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/38)).

**About 35 new template variables**

Meetings Plus now reads much more of each calendar event: who accepted or declined, required vs optional attendees, booked rooms, the organizer's email, the calendar's own Teams/Meet/Zoom link, categories, privacy, the recurrence rule in plain English ("Every 2 weeks on Mon, Wed"), reminders, attachments and more. A few favourites: `{{attendees_table}}`, `{{organizer_email}}`, `{{duration_hm}}`, `{{end_date}}` and `{{recurrence}}`. The full list is in the README and in the template help. Existing templates render exactly as before. Thanks @mrueg ([#39](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/39)).

**Hide declined, out-of-office and free events**

Each calendar now has optional filters to hide meetings you've declined, out-of-office blocks and events marked free. They apply everywhere: sidebar, daily note and notifications. To hide declined meetings, enter your email address in the calendar's settings. All filters are off by default. Thanks @mrueg ([#40](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/40)).

**Daily notes use your template**

When Meetings Plus creates today's daily note, it now goes through Obsidian's Daily notes plugin, so your daily-note template is applied. Thanks @jarno-lernou-skyline ([#35](https://github.com/jabaho9523/obsidian-meetings-plus/pull/35)) — and welcome!

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. Settings, calendars, templates and existing notes carry over, and the new filters start switched off.
