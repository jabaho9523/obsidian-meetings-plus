**Cleaner daily notes while editing**

Meeting sections in your daily note are wrapped in small hidden markers. That's how Meetings Plus finds a section again, so it never duplicates or overwrites it. Reading view always hid them, but in Live Preview they showed up as long grey lines, especially with Outlook's long event IDs.

They're now hidden in Live Preview as well. Put your cursor on the line and the marker reappears, the same way Obsidian hides `**bold**` or `[[link]]` syntax until you click into it. Nothing in your files changes.

Prefer to see them? Turn off **Settings → Meetings Plus → Hide section markers in the editor**.

**Upgrading**

Nothing to do. Settings, calendars and templates carry over untouched, and the new setting is on by default.
